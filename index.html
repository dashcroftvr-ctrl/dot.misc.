Gav

How do I use it in the headset? Animation menu floats to your left. Press a button with your fingertip, or point a controller (or pinch) and pull the trigger.
Thumbstick left/right = previous/next animation. Grip = hide/show the menu.
Point at Gav and pull the trigger (or pinch) = next animation.
Bring here puts Gav back in front of you and moves the menu beside you.
On the spot stops walking/running moves carrying him across your room — turn it off to let him roam.
